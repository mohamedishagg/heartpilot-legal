1. Who we are

HeartPilot is developed and operated in the United Arab Emirates. This Privacy Policy explains what information HeartPilot ("we," "our," "the app") collects when you use our mobile application, why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using HeartPilot, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Email address and password (used to create and secure your account)
  • A unique account identifier generated automatically

2.2 Health and self-monitoring information you provide

To provide the app's core function, we collect health information you enter directly, including:

  • Daily check-in data: body weight, breathlessness, swelling, fatigue, orthopnea, palpitations, blood pressure, and heart rate
  • Medication names, doses, schedules, and whether doses were marked taken, skipped, or missed
  • Appointment and lab test reminders you create
  • Monthly wellbeing check answers (mood screening questions, sleep quality, activity level)
  • Personalized care plan information (such as fluid or sodium guidance) if entered by you or your care team
  • Messages or indications you provide when using the "I need help now" or similar safety features

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device timezone (used to schedule reminders and alerts at appropriate local times)
  • Push notification token (used to deliver alerts to your device)
  • Basic technical information such as app version, device type, and operating system, used for crash reporting and reliability

3. Apple Health and Health Connect data

If you choose to enable it, HeartPilot can read certain data from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), specifically: body weight, blood pressure, heart rate, and step count. This is entirely optional and controlled by permissions you grant through your device's operating system, which you can revoke at any time in your device settings.

HeartPilot only reads this data. It does not write, modify, or delete any data in Apple Health or Health Connect. Data read from Apple Health or Health Connect is used solely to pre-fill your daily check-in and is stored within HeartPilot in the same way as data you enter manually.

In accordance with Apple's Developer Program guidelines, health data obtained through HealthKit is not used for advertising or other use-based data mining purposes, and is not shared with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or information resellers.

4. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide your daily check-in, medication reminders, and appointment reminders
  • Run automated, rules-based checks on your check-in data (a "red-flag" system) to surface guidance such as "contact your clinic" or "seek emergency help," based on thresholds informed by published heart failure guidelines
  • Notify a caregiver you have explicitly invited, according to the permissions you have granted them (see Section 5)
  • Detect and respond to prolonged inactivity in your check-ins, so that a caregiver can be notified if you appear to have stopped engaging with the app
  • Display educational content relevant to heart failure self-management
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the app

We do not use your health information for advertising. HeartPilot does not sell personal or health information, and does not use it to serve targeted advertisements.

5. Caregiver sharing

HeartPilot allows you to invite a family member or other person as a "caregiver" using a one-time invite code that you generate and share yourself. Before you send an invite, the app shows you exactly what information a caregiver will be able to see.

Depending on the permissions you grant, a caregiver may be able to see: whether you have completed your daily check-in, your current status (stable, needs attention, or emergency) from your most recent check-in, your weight trend, and your medication adherence.

For wellbeing (mood) checks specifically: a caregiver is only ever notified that a check-in has occurred and that you may benefit from a conversation. A caregiver never sees your specific wellbeing answers or score.

You may revoke a caregiver's access at any time from within the app. Revocation takes effect immediately, and the caregiver will no longer be able to view any of your information from that point onward.

7. Data retention

We retain your account and health information for as long as your account remains active, so that your check-in history remains useful for you and your care team. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by applicable law or necessary to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

8. Security

We apply technical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access-control rules that restrict your health data so that only you, and any caregiver you have explicitly authorized, can read it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You can:

  • View your own check-in and medication history at any time within the app
  • Revoke a caregiver's access at any time
  • Disable Apple Health / Health Connect access at any time from your device settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below

10. Children's privacy

HeartPilot is intended for adults managing their own heart failure care, or for use by an adult caregiver. It is not directed at, and should not be used by, children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International data transfers

HeartPilot is operated from the United Arab Emirates. Our service providers may process and store data in other countries, including the United States. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards provided by our service providers' own compliance frameworks.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you within the app or by other reasonable means before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
